# NEET PG depth for the short clinical subjects.
== ophthalmology
@ Anatomy & optics
+ Refraction errors: myopia; hypermetropia; astigmatism; anisometropia; correction with lenses
+ Visual acuity testing: Snellen chart; LogMAR; near vision; pinhole test
@ Anterior segment
+ Conjunctiva: bacterial, viral and allergic conjunctivitis; trachoma (SAFE strategy); pterygium; vernal keratoconjunctivitis
+ Cornea: bacterial keratitis; fungal keratitis; Acanthamoeba keratitis; keratoconus; corneal transplant
+ Uvea: anterior uveitis; posterior uveitis; HLA-B27 uveitis; sympathetic ophthalmia
+ Dry eye: Schirmer test; tear break-up time; Sjögren syndrome
@ Glaucoma
+ Secondary glaucoma: neovascular; phacomorphic and phacolytic; steroid-induced; uveitic glaucoma
+ Anti-glaucoma drugs: prostaglandin analogues; beta blockers; carbonic anhydrase inhibitors; alpha agonists; pilocarpine; mannitol in acute angle closure
+ Glaucoma surgery: laser peripheral iridotomy; trabeculectomy; glaucoma drainage devices
@ Retina & optic nerve
+ Retinoblastoma: leukocoria; RB1 gene; management
+ Retinitis pigmentosa: night blindness; bone-spicule pigmentation; ring scotoma
+ ARMD: dry vs wet AMD; drusen; anti-VEGF therapy
+ Retinopathy of prematurity: risk factors; zones and stages; screening criteria; laser and anti-VEGF
@ Squint, orbit & community
+ Blindness programmes: NPCB&VI; definitions of blindness; Vision 2020; common causes in India
+ Neuro-ophthalmology: optic neuritis; papilloedema; cranial nerve palsies (III, IV, VI); pupillary abnormalities
+ Ocular trauma: blunt trauma and hyphaema; penetrating injury; chemical injury; sympathetic ophthalmia
+ Eye in systemic disease: diabetic retinopathy; hypertensive retinopathy; thyroid eye disease; eye in HIV

== ent
@ Ear
+ Otitis media & complications: acute otitis media; CSOM (mucosal vs squamous); intratemporal complications; intracranial complications
+ Hearing loss & tests: tuning fork tests (Rinne, Weber); pure tone audiometry; impedance audiometry; BERA and OAE
+ Vertigo: BPPV and Dix-Hallpike; Ménière disease; vestibular neuritis; central vs peripheral vertigo
+ Otosclerosis: Schwartze sign; Carhart notch; stapedectomy
+ Facial nerve palsy: Bell palsy; Ramsay Hunt syndrome; House-Brackmann grading
+ Cholesteatoma: pathogenesis; attic perforation; mastoidectomy
+ Cochlear implants: candidacy; components; outcomes
@ Nose & sinuses
+ Epistaxis: Little area; anterior vs posterior epistaxis; nasal packing; SPA ligation
+ Sinusitis: acute and chronic rhinosinusitis; complications; FESS
+ Nasal polyps & tumours: ethmoidal vs antrochoanal polyp; inverted papilloma; sinonasal malignancy
+ DNS & rhinitis: deviated nasal septum and septoplasty; allergic rhinitis; atrophic rhinitis
+ Fungal sinusitis: allergic fungal sinusitis; invasive mucormycosis
+ Juvenile nasopharyngeal angiofibroma: adolescent males; Holman-Miller sign; embolisation and surgery
+ CSF rhinorrhoea: beta-2 transferrin; endoscopic repair
@ Pharynx & larynx
+ Tonsils & adenoids: tonsillitis; peritonsillar abscess (quinsy); adenoid hypertrophy; tonsillectomy indications
+ Laryngeal disorders: vocal nodules and polyps; laryngeal papillomatosis; laryngeal carcinoma
+ Tracheostomy: indications; types; complications; care
+ Stridor: laryngomalacia; subglottic stenosis; acute epiglottitis; croup
+ Vocal cord palsy: recurrent laryngeal nerve palsy; positions of the cord; management
+ Deep neck space infections: retropharyngeal abscess; parapharyngeal abscess; Ludwig angina
+ Oesophageal foreign bodies: coin vs button battery; oesophagoscopy

== orthopaedics
@ Trauma
+ Fracture principles: fracture healing; classification; reduction and fixation; complications
+ Dislocations: shoulder dislocation; elbow dislocation; hip dislocation; reduction techniques
+ Pelvic fractures: Young-Burgess classification; haemorrhage control; urethral injury
+ Hand injuries: Bennett and Rolando fractures; boxer fracture; mallet finger
+ Compartment syndrome: 5 Ps; compartment pressure; fasciotomy
@ Infections & tumours
+ Osteomyelitis & bone TB: acute haematogenous osteomyelitis; chronic osteomyelitis and sequestrum; Brodie abscess; tuberculous arthritis
+ Bone tumours: benign vs malignant features; staging; biopsy principles
+ Septic arthritis: organisms; Kocher criteria; arthrotomy
+ Spinal TB: Pott spine; cold abscess; Pott paraplegia
+ Osteosarcoma & Ewing's: Codman triangle; sunburst appearance; onion-peel periosteal reaction; chemotherapy
+ Giant cell tumour: epiphyseal location; soap-bubble appearance; curettage and bone grafting
@ Regional & paediatric orthopaedics
+ Peripheral nerve injuries: Seddon and Sunderland grading; nerve repair; tendon transfers
+ Metabolic bone disease: rickets; osteomalacia; renal osteodystrophy; Paget disease
+ Sports injuries: ACL tear; meniscal tear; ankle sprain; shoulder dislocation
+ Osteoarthritis & RA: OA radiology; RA deformities; arthroplasty
+ Low back pain: disc prolapse; lumbar canal stenosis; red flags
+ Perthes & SCFE: Catterall and Herring classification; Trethowan sign; fixation in situ
+ Rickets & osteoporosis: radiological signs of rickets; DEXA T-score; fragility fractures
+ Amputations & prosthetics: levels of amputation; prosthetic fitting

== dermatology
@ Basics & infections
+ Leprosy: Ridley-Jopling classification; lepra reactions; MDT regimens; slit-skin smear
+ STIs: syphilis stages; chancroid; LGV; donovanosis; gonorrhoea; syndromic management
+ Viral infections of skin: herpes simplex; herpes zoster; warts; molluscum contagiosum; HFMD
+ Skin biopsy & investigations: punch biopsy; Tzanck smear; KOH mount; Wood lamp; patch test
@ Inflammatory & immunobullous
+ Vesiculobullous disorders: pemphigus vulgaris; bullous pemphigoid; dermatitis herpetiformis; Nikolsky sign
+ Pigmentary & hair disorders: vitiligo; melasma; alopecia areata; androgenetic alopecia
+ Cutaneous malignancies: BCC; SCC; melanoma (ABCDE); mycosis fungoides

== psychiatry
@ Major disorders
+ Schizophrenia: positive and negative symptoms; Schneider first-rank symptoms; subtypes; antipsychotic treatment
+ Anxiety & OCD: generalised anxiety disorder; panic disorder; phobias; OCD and SSRIs
+ Bipolar disorder: mania criteria; mood stabilisers; lithium monitoring
+ Somatoform & dissociative disorders: somatisation; conversion disorder; dissociative amnesia
+ Eating disorders: anorexia nervosa; bulimia nervosa; refeeding syndrome
+ Sleep disorders: insomnia; narcolepsy; sleep apnoea; parasomnias
+ Alcohol dependence: CAGE questionnaire; withdrawal and delirium tremens; Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome
@ Other topics
+ Personality disorders: cluster A, B and C; borderline personality disorder
+ Mental Healthcare Act: 2017 Act; advance directive; decriminalisation of suicide attempt
+ ECT: indications; contraindications; side effects
+ Defence mechanisms: mature; neurotic; immature defences
+ Delirium & dementia: delirium vs dementia; causes of delirium; management

== radiology
@ Physics & safety
+ Contrast media: iodinated contrast; gadolinium; contrast reactions; contrast nephropathy
+ Nuclear medicine: radionuclides; bone scan; thyroid scan; PET-CT
@ Imaging signs
+ Chest X-ray signs: silhouette sign; air bronchogram; deep sulcus sign; golden S sign; cardiomegaly
+ Abdominal imaging: pneumoperitoneum; bowel obstruction; coffee bean sign; double bubble sign
+ Neuroimaging: CT in stroke; MRI sequences; ring-enhancing lesions
+ Radiotherapy basics: radiosensitivity; fractionation; brachytherapy vs teletherapy
+ Musculoskeletal imaging: periosteal reactions; bone tumour signs; fracture imaging
+ Paediatric radiology: intussusception; NEC; hypertrophic pyloric stenosis
+ Interventional radiology: angioplasty; embolisation; drainage procedures
+ Imaging in trauma: FAST; trauma CT; cervical spine imaging

== anaesthesia
@ General anaesthesia
+ Pre-op assessment: ASA grading; airway assessment (Mallampati); fasting guidelines
+ Airway management: bag-mask ventilation; LMA; intubation; difficult airway algorithm
+ Inhalational & IV agents: MAC; nitrous oxide; sevoflurane; propofol; ketamine; thiopentone
+ Muscle relaxants: succinylcholine; non-depolarising agents; reversal
+ Anaesthesia machine: safety features; colour coding of cylinders; breathing circuits
+ Malignant hyperthermia: triggers; RYR1 mutation; dantrolene
+ Post-operative nausea & pain: PONV risk scores; multimodal analgesia
@ Regional & critical care
+ Spinal & epidural: differences; level of block; complications (PDPH, hypotension)
+ Local anaesthetics: amides vs esters; maximum doses; LAST and lipid emulsion
+ CPR & BLS/ACLS: chain of survival; compression-ventilation ratio; shockable rhythms; drugs in ACLS
+ Nerve blocks: brachial plexus block; femoral block; Bier block
+ Oxygen therapy: delivery devices; oxygen toxicity
+ Mechanical ventilation basics: modes; PEEP; ventilator settings
+ Fluid therapy: perioperative fluids; blood loss replacement
