== anaesthesia.paper
@ Anesthesia physiology, pharmacology and preassessment
! Official DNB Anaesthesiology syllabus — first- and second-year theory.
+ Applied anatomy for anaesthesia: heart and coronary circulation; bronchopulmonary segments; larynx and airway; diaphragm; vertebral column and spinal canal; nerve plexuses
+ Applied biochemistry: perioperative fluid therapy; blood transfusion and artificial blood; acid-base and electrolytes
+ Anaesthesia history and practice: history of anaesthesia in India; documentation and medicolegal aspects; informed consent and record keeping; PCPNDT rules for ultrasound use
@ Anesthetic techniques and monitoring
+ Anaesthesia machine and equipment: workstation check; vaporisers; breathing systems and valves; flowmeters and pressure-reducing valves; humidifiers; medical gas pipeline and scavenging; ventilator principles; sterilisation of equipment
+ Monitoring standards: minimum monitoring standards; cardiac output and pressure monitoring; depth of anaesthesia; neuromuscular monitoring; equipment safety
+ Neuraxial anaesthesia: spinal (midline, paramedian, lumbosacral); epidural (interlaminar, caudal, thoracic); combined spinal-epidural; complications and conversion to GA
+ Peripheral and fascial plane blocks: brachial plexus (all approaches); cervical plexus; stellate ganglion; intercostal and paravertebral; PECS I and II; erector spinae; quadratus lumborum; TAP and rectus sheath; ilioinguinal; sciatic and femoral (3-in-1); IVRA; nerve stimulator and ultrasound guidance
+ Point-of-care ultrasound: regional anaesthesia; FAST and lung ultrasound; vascular access; airway ultrasound
+ Positioning and theatre safety: positioning injuries; diathermy burns; induced hypotension
@ Anesthesia for major specialties
+ Anaesthesia outside the theatre: day-care anaesthesia; MRI and radiology suite; ECT; endoscopy and ERCP; IVF; dental; remote locations and high altitude; monitored anaesthesia care
+ Special patient groups: cardiac, respiratory, renal, hepatic and endocrine disease for non-cardiac surgery; one-lung anaesthesia; bariatric anaesthesia; transplant anaesthesia; burns and plastic surgery; geriatric anaesthesia; robotic surgery
@ Anesthesia intensive care and resuscitation
+ Resuscitation and recovery: BLS and ACLS; cardiac vs thoracic pump theory; delayed recovery from anaesthesia; PACU care; shock management
+ Pulmonary function testing: bedside PFT; laboratory PFT interpretation
@ Anaesthesia non-technical skills
! New chapter — official DNB Anaesthesiology syllabus, non-technical skills.
+ Professional skills: crisis resource management; communication and conflict resolution; medical audit; human resource and material management; health policy
