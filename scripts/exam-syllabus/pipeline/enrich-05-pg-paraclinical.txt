# NEET PG para-clinical depth: high-yield subtopics for topics that had none (NMC CBME UG scope).
== pathology
@ Cell injury & adaptation
+ Necrosis & apoptosis: coagulative necrosis; liquefactive necrosis; caseous necrosis; fat necrosis; fibrinoid necrosis; intrinsic vs extrinsic apoptosis; BCL-2 family and caspases
+ Cellular adaptations: hypertrophy; hyperplasia; atrophy; metaplasia (Barrett, squamous)
+ Intracellular accumulations: fatty change; cholesterol and cholesterol esters; hyaline change; glycogen; lipofuscin; haemosiderin
+ Calcification: dystrophic calcification; metastatic calcification; psammoma bodies
+ Free radical injury: reactive oxygen species; ischaemia-reperfusion injury; antioxidant defences; chemical (CCl4) injury
+ Reversible vs irreversible injury: cell swelling; mitochondrial damage; membrane damage; point of no return
+ Autophagy: mechanism; role in disease
+ Cellular ageing: telomere shortening; replicative senescence; progeria
@ Inflammation & repair
+ Acute inflammation & mediators: vascular changes; outcomes of acute inflammation; morphological patterns (serous, fibrinous, purulent, ulcer)
+ Chronic & granulomatous inflammation: macrophages and lymphocytes; epithelioid granuloma; caseating vs non-caseating; causes of granulomas
+ Wound healing: healing by first and second intention; growth factors; keloid and hypertrophic scar; factors affecting healing
+ Leukocyte recruitment: margination and rolling (selectins); adhesion (integrins); transmigration (PECAM-1); chemotaxis; leukocyte adhesion deficiency
+ Chemical mediators: histamine and serotonin; arachidonic acid metabolites; cytokines (TNF, IL-1); complement; bradykinin; nitric oxide
+ Systemic effects of inflammation: fever; acute-phase reactants; leukocytosis; sepsis and SIRS
+ Fibrosis: TGF-β; scar remodelling; cirrhosis and pulmonary fibrosis as examples
@ Haemodynamics
+ Oedema & thrombosis: transudate vs exudate; Virchow triad; arterial vs venous thrombi; lines of Zahn; fate of thrombus
+ Embolism & infarction: pulmonary embolism; fat embolism; amniotic fluid embolism; air embolism; red vs white infarcts
+ Shock: hypovolaemic; cardiogenic; septic; stages of shock; morphology of shock
+ Hyperaemia & congestion: nutmeg liver; heart failure cells; congestion of lung and spleen
+ DIC: triggers; consumptive coagulopathy; laboratory profile
+ Haemorrhage: petechiae, purpura and ecchymosis; haematoma
@ Immunopathology
+ Hypersensitivity reactions: type I (anaphylaxis); type II (antibody-mediated); type III (immune complex); type IV (delayed); examples of each
+ Autoimmune diseases: mechanisms of tolerance loss; autoantibodies (ANA, anti-dsDNA, anti-CCP); organ-specific vs systemic
+ Amyloidosis: AL and AA amyloid; Congo red with apple-green birefringence; organ involvement; dialysis-related and familial amyloid
+ Transplant rejection: hyperacute; acute cellular and humoral; chronic rejection
+ Immunodeficiency: Bruton agammaglobulinaemia; DiGeorge syndrome; SCID; Wiskott-Aldrich; common variable immunodeficiency
+ MHC & HLA: class I and II; HLA disease associations (B27, DR3, DR4)
+ Graft-versus-host disease: acute and chronic GVHD; target organs
+ AIDS pathology: HIV life cycle; CD4 depletion; opportunistic infections; AIDS-related tumours
+ SLE pathology: lupus nephritis classes; LE cells; libman-Sacks endocarditis; skin lesions
@ Neoplasia
